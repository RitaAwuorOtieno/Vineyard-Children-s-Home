Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information about supporting Vineyard Children's Home.

How can I make a donation?

Visit the donation page for M-PESA, bank transfer, and in-kind giving options.

Donation options
Can I donate goods instead of money?

Food staples, clothing, school supplies, bedding, and hygiene items are welcome. Please call ahead to coordinate delivery.

Call 0713 019 917
How do I volunteer?

Call the home to discuss volunteering opportunities. Visits and volunteering arrangements are made in advance and follow the home's child-safeguarding guidance.

Volunteer information
How can I support children's needs?

Call the home to discuss ways to support children's wellbeing, learning, and everyday needs. Children's identities and privacy are protected.

Call 0713 019 917
What is the college project?

The college and skills-centre is a proposed long-term initiative to create practical learning opportunities for young people.

Learn about the project
Can I visit the home?

Visits are arranged in advance to protect children's safety, privacy, and daily routines. Please do not arrive without an appointment or take photographs without permission.

Arrange a visit
Can a group or organisation partner with the home?

We welcome conversations about suitable partnerships and community support.
