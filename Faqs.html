Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information about supporting Vineyard Children's Home.

How can I make a donation?

Visit our donation page for the available M-PESA, bank transfer, and in-kind options. Confirm payment details with the home before making a transfer.

Donation options
Can I donate goods instead of money?

Yes. Food, clothing, school supplies, bedding, and hygiene items may be useful. Please contact us first so we can confirm current needs and arrange a suitable delivery time.

How do I volunteer?

Start with the volunteer form on our Get Involved page. Volunteers are screened and must follow the home's child safeguarding, privacy, and media guidelines.

Volunteer information
How does child sponsorship work?

Contact the home to discuss approved ways to support children's needs. We protect children's identities and do not arrange unsupervised or direct access to children.

What is the college project?

The long-term vision is to establish a college and vocational skills centre that can help young adults develop practical skills. Details and timelines will be shared when confirmed.

Learn about the project
Can I visit the home?

Visits must be arranged in advance with the team. This helps us protect children's safety, privacy, and daily routines. Please do not arrive without an appointment or take photographs without permission.

Arrange an enquiry
Can a group or organisation partner with the home?

We welcome conversations with organisations, schools, churches, and community groups about appropriate partnerships. Contact us to discuss your idea and our current priorities.

Need more information?

Send us a message and our team will respond when available.
