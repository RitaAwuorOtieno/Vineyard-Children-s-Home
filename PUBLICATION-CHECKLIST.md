# Publication checklist

The site owner should supply or approve the following before the corresponding content or functionality is published. Do not put credentials, private child records, or confidential volunteer/donor information in this checklist.

## Organization details

- [ ] Official public telephone number and permission to publish it.
- [ ] Official public email address and a monitored inbox.
- [ ] Approved public address and any map location to display.
- [ ] Confirmed organization history, mission, vision, and values.
- [ ] Review and approve the proposed About page purpose, mission, vision, and values wording before publication.
- [ ] Legal/registration details, only if verified and approved for publication.
- [ ] Official leadership names, roles, and biographies, only with approval.

## Public content

- [ ] Confirm which programmes are currently active and approve their descriptions.
- [ ] Confirm completed achievements, statistics, sources, and dates.
- [ ] Approve any anonymized story and confirm its facts and authorization.
- [ ] Confirm college project status, scope, funding claims, and timeline; label proposals as proposed.
- [ ] Supply approved social media URLs.
- [ ] Supply authentic photographs with documented authorization and safeguarding review; provide non-identifying alt text and captions.
- [ ] Review and approve privacy and child-safeguarding/photography notices.

## Donations and external services

- [ ] Independently verify the M-PESA till, bank, account name, account number, branch and bank codes, and SWIFT details before publication.
- [ ] Confirm in-kind donation needs and approved delivery arrangements.
- [ ] Configure the Vineyard Firebase project, deploy reviewed Firestore and Storage rules, set authorized website domains and exact Storage CORS origins, and complete the administrator-only access tests.
- [ ] Decide whether to enable Cloud Storage and link a billing account after reviewing Blaze pricing and budget alerts.
- [ ] Configure Firebase App Check before relying on public enquiry submissions; confirm spam protection, access controls, retention, deletion, and privacy disclosures for enquiries.
- [ ] If a payment provider is selected, configure it through the provider's secure hosted checkout and test it; do not collect card details in this static site.
- [ ] Confirm hosting ownership, HTTPS, deployment permissions, and authorized repository maintainers.

## Final release

- [ ] Replace any remaining unverified content only with approved facts.
- [ ] Test all pages, links, forms, mobile navigation, keyboard interaction, and deployed assets on the production host.
- [ ] Confirm no private data, credentials, or unauthorized child images are committed or publicly served.
