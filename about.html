About Vineyard Children's Home

Learn about our history, mission, vision, and the values that guide everything we do.

Our Story

Vineyard Children's Home was established to provide a safe and loving home for orphaned and vulnerable children. What began as a small act of compassion has grown into a thriving community dedicated to transforming young lives.

Founded with the belief that every child deserves dignity, care, and opportunity, we have grown into a registered charitable organization serving children from diverse backgrounds.

Our Mission

To provide holistic care, education, and support to vulnerable children - nurturing them into confident, self-reliant individuals who contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

A world where every child has a safe home, quality education, and the opportunity to build a bright and independent future.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion We care for every child with love, empathy, and respect.
  • Integrity We are transparent, accountable, and ethical in all we do.
  • Excellence We strive for the highest standards in care and education.
  • Empowerment We equip children with skills for lifelong independence.
  • Community We work together with families, partners, and supporters.
  • Dignity We honour the worth and potential of every child.

What Makes Us Different

Our approach goes beyond basic care - we build futures.

Family-Like Environment

Children grow up in a warm, nurturing setting that feels like home, not an institution.

Long-Term Focus

We walk with each child from childhood through to independence and career readiness.

Skills for Life

Beyond academics, we teach practical and entrepreneurial skills for self-sustenance.
