About Vineyard Children's Home

A welcoming place where every child is treated with care, dignity, and respect.

About Us

Vineyard Children Centre is a registered Christian charitable organization gazetted as a voluntary society under the Children Act (Cap. 586 of the Laws of Kenya).

Since December 2017, Vineyard Children Home has provided residential care in Komarock Ward, Kwanguruwe.

The home provides care and protection to children who are orphaned, abandoned, neglected, or otherwise vulnerable.

Children at the Centre have opportunities to access education, shelter, nutrition, medication, and other support related to their rights and wellbeing.

Our mission

Our mission is to support children in a loving, nurturing environment where their basic needs are met and they can receive parental care, spiritual guidance, education, and rehabilitation support.

We aim to ensure that children who cannot live with their relatives have food, shelter, opportunities for play and leisure, and care in a supportive environment.

Our vision

Our vision is for children to thrive and reach their potential in a safe, loving environment, with support for education, nutrition, medication, and mentorship as they grow into responsible members of their communities.

What guides us

The principles behind a respectful, child-centred approach to care.

  • CompassionMeet each child with patience, warmth, and understanding.
  • DignityRespect every child's voice, privacy, and individual story.
  • SafetyMake children's protection and wellbeing central to decisions.
  • OpportunityEncourage learning, confidence, and personal growth.

Leadership

Our team works together to provide consistent care and support, guided by compassion, respect, and a commitment to children's wellbeing.
