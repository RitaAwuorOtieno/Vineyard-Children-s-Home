About Vineyard Children's Home

A welcoming place where every child is treated with care, dignity, and respect.

About Us

Vineyard Children Centre is a duly registered Christian charitable organization approved through gazette notice as a voluntary society, under the terms of children act(Cap 586,Law of Kenya).

Vineyard Children Centre has a residential structure where children reside from (Vineyard Children Home) in Komarock ward, Kwanguruwe slum since its enception in December 2017.

Many neglected, abandoned, orphans and vulnerable children being intergrated in the Home for care and protection.

The children rescued are as a result of mistreat, agony, abuse, family conflict and dispute, violence and neglect. The children at the Centre get opportunities to better education, shelter, nutrition, medication and other livelihood child rights

Our mission

To support children by providing a loving and nurturing environment for their well-being to have their basic needs, parental care, spiritual guidance, education and well rehabilitation.

"Our aim is to make sure that the children who can not live with their own kinship under our care their needs are met and we proide food, shelter, place to play and leisure in a caring environment".

We make sure the children grow and develop.

Our vision

To support children to thrive and realize their full potential by providing a safe, loving environment and to ensure that they receive support needed for their education, Nutrition, Medication and Mentorship to be responsible and able to bring development in societies they come from.

What guides us

The principles behind a respectful, child-centred approach to care.

  • CompassionMeet each child with patience, warmth, and understanding.
  • DignityRespect every child's voice, privacy, and individual story.
  • SafetyMake children's protection and wellbeing central to decisions.
  • OpportunityEncourage learning, confidence, and personal growth.

Leadership

Our team works together to provide consistent care and support, guided by compassion, respect, and a commitment to children's wellbeing.
