About Vineyard Children's Home

A welcoming place where every child is treated with care, dignity, and respect.

Our purpose

Vineyard Children's Home exists to offer children a safe, caring environment where their wellbeing, dignity, and individual potential come first.

We believe children deserve to be listened to, encouraged, and supported as they learn and grow. Our approach should be guided by each child's needs and respect for their identity, privacy, and rights.

Our mission

To provide compassionate care and encouragement that helps children feel safe, valued, and able to pursue a hopeful future.

Our vision

A future in which every child can grow with security, dignity, and the opportunity to reach their potential.

What guides us

The principles behind a respectful, child-centred approach to care.

  • CompassionMeet each child with patience, warmth, and understanding.
  • DignityRespect every child's voice, privacy, and individual story.
  • SafetyMake children's protection and wellbeing central to decisions.
  • OpportunityEncourage learning, confidence, and personal growth.

Leadership

Our team works together to provide consistent care and support, guided by compassion, respect, and a commitment to children's wellbeing.
