Our Future College & Skills Centre

Equipping young adults with practical skills to build independent, self-sustaining futures.

The Challenge We Face

Many of our children complete high school with dreams of continuing to college - but due to insufficient funds, they are unable to pursue higher education. Without further training or skills, they face an uncertain future and the risk of unemployment and poverty.

We believe this cycle must be broken. Every child who leaves our home should be equipped to stand on their own two feet and build a meaningful, productive life.

Our Vision: A College & Skills Training Centre

We are planning to establish our own college and vocational training centre on our premises. This centre will offer practical, market-relevant skills that empower our young people to start and run small businesses - creating sustainable livelihoods for themselves and their communities.

Proposed Skills & Courses

Practical training designed for real-world success and entrepreneurship.

Technical Trades

Carpentry, welding, plumbing, electrical installation, and mechanics.

ICT & Digital Skills

Computer literacy, web design, graphic design, and digital marketing.

Fashion & Textiles

Tailoring, dressmaking, embroidery, and fashion design.

Catering & Hospitality

Cooking, baking, food processing, and hospitality management.

Agriculture & Agribusiness

Modern farming, poultry, horticulture, and agribusiness management.

Business & Entrepreneurship

Business planning, financial literacy, marketing, and small business management.

Beauty & Cosmetology

Hairdressing, barbering, makeup, and salon management.

Construction

Masonry, tiling, painting, and building construction skills.

Automotive

Motor vehicle mechanics, driving, and auto repair.

Why This Matters

Breaks the Cycle

Skills training breaks the cycle of poverty and dependency.

Creates Jobs

Graduates can start businesses and employ others in their communities.

Builds Independence

Young adults gain the ability to support themselves and their future families.
