A Future Full of Possibility

We want every young person leaving our care to have the skills, confidence, and opportunity to shape their own future.

Our vision

Vineyard Children's Home is working toward a future where children and young adults can move from care into safe, independent, and meaningful lives. Education, practical skills, and supportive relationships are central to that future.

Our long-term vision includes a college and vocational skills centre that can equip young people with relevant training and open pathways to employment and entrepreneurship. This is a developing vision; programme details and timelines will be shared as plans are confirmed.

What we are building toward

A practical, child-centred approach to helping young people thrive.

📚

Learning that opens doors

Support children to access education and keep building knowledge, confidence, and curiosity.

🛠️

Skills for independence

Develop vocational and life skills that help young adults pursue work, further study, or enterprise.

🤝

A community of support

Work with families, educators, local organisations, and supporters to create lasting opportunities.

How you can help shape this future

Your support can help provide education, mentorship, essential care, and the resources needed to plan for future training opportunities. Organisations and individuals are welcome to discuss skills partnerships, equipment, mentorship, or project support with our team.
