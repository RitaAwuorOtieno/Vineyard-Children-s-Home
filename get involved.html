Volunteer Guidelines

To ensure the safety and wellbeing of our children, all volunteers must:

Complete an application and screening process
Provide references and undergo background checks
Follow our child safeguarding and media policies
Respect children's privacy and confidentiality
Arrange visits in advance with our coordinator
