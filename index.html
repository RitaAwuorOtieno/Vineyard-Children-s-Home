Welcome to Vineyard Children's Home

A safe haven where children grow, learn, and thrive.

Vineyard Children's Home is a registered charitable organization dedicated to providing holistic care for orphaned and vulnerable children. We believe every child deserves love, dignity, education, and the opportunity to reach their full potential.

Our home offers residential care, education support, healthcare, nutrition, and skills development - all within a nurturing family-like environment. We are committed to raising confident, self-reliant young people who will positively impact their communities.

What We Do

Comprehensive care that addresses every aspect of a child's wellbeing.

Residential Care

A safe, loving home environment where children receive shelter, nutritious meals, and daily care.

Education Support

School fees, learning materials, tutoring, and mentorship to help every child succeed academically.

Healthcare

Routine medical care, health checkups, and access to partner clinics and hospitals.

Nutrition

Balanced daily meals that support healthy growth and development for every child.

Skills & Talent

Arts, sports, music, and vocational skills that nurture creativity and confidence.

Counselling & Mentorship

Emotional support and guidance to help children heal and build resilience.

Our Future: A College & Skills Centre

Preparing young adults for independence through practical skills and entrepreneurship.

Many of our children complete high school but cannot afford college. We are working to build our own college and vocational training centre where they can learn practical, income-generating skills - equipping them to start and run small businesses and become self-reliant members of society.
