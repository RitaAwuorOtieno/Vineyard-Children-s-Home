Care and support

Vineyard Children Centre describes a residential home providing care and protection for children in Komarock Ward, Kwanguruwe.

Areas of care

These areas reflect the services and support described by the home.

Residential care and protection

The home provides a place to live and care for children who need protection. Its stated approach includes shelter, parental care, and a caring environment.

Education and learning

Children are supported to access education and develop their knowledge and abilities.

Health, nutrition, and daily needs

Support includes basic needs such as food, shelter, nutrition, and medication.

Spiritual guidance and recreation

The home’s stated mission includes spiritual guidance. Children also have opportunities for play and leisure.

Proposed college and skills centre

Vineyard Children's Home has proposed a college and skills centre to create practical learning opportunities for young people.
