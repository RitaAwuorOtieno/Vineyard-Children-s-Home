Programmes

Care, encouragement, and opportunity can help children build confidence and look ahead with hope.

A caring foundation for every child

Every child deserves to feel safe, valued, and listened to. A supportive home should make room for each child's needs, personality, and hopes for the future.

We believe care, learning, health, and personal growth belong together. With encouragement and steady support, children can build confidence, discover their strengths, and prepare for new opportunities.

Future initiative

Vineyard Children's Home has a vision for a college and skills centre where young people could develop practical skills for the future. This is a proposed initiative; its plans are still taking shape.
