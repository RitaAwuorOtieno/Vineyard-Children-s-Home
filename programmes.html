Our Programs & Services

Holistic care that meets the physical, emotional, educational, and developmental needs of every child.

Residential Care

We provide a safe, stable, and loving home environment for children who have lost parental care or are at risk. Our residential program ensures every child has:

Safe shelter and a comfortable place to sleep
Three nutritious meals daily
Clean clothing and personal hygiene supplies
24/7 supervision and care from trained staff
A family-like atmosphere that promotes belonging

Education Support

Education is the foundation of a bright future. We support our children's learning through:

School fees and educational materials
Uniforms, books, and stationery
After-school tutoring and homework help
Access to a library and learning resources
Computer literacy training
Career guidance and mentorship

Healthcare & Nutrition

Healthy children learn better and grow stronger. Our health program includes:

Routine medical checkups
Access to partner clinics and hospitals
Immunizations and preventive care
Balanced, nutritious daily meals
Hygiene and sanitation education
Emotional and psychological support

Skills & Talent Development

Nurturing creativity, confidence, and practical abilities.

Sports & Recreation

Football, athletics, and other sports that build teamwork, discipline, and healthy living.

Music & Arts

Choir, dance, drama, and visual arts that allow children to express themselves creatively.

Computer Skills

Digital literacy and basic computer training to prepare children for the modern world.

Vocational Skills

Practical training in trades and income-generating skills for future self-reliance.

Agriculture

Gardening and farming projects that teach responsibility and food production.

Life Skills

Financial literacy, communication, and decision-making skills for independent living.

Counselling & Mentorship

Many of our children have experienced trauma and loss. We provide:

Individual and group counselling sessions
Trained counsellors and social workers
Peer mentorship programs
Spiritual and emotional guidance
A safe space to heal and grow

Family & Community Support

We believe in strengthening families and communities. Our outreach includes:

Family reunification and reintegration support
Community awareness and child protection
Support for vulnerable families
Collaboration with local schools and churches
